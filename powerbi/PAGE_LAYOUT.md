# Power BI page layout

Canvas 16:9 (1280 x 720). Theme colours: navy `#1F3A5F` (IBM), teal `#2A9D8F` (UCI),
orange `#E76F51` (SIMULATED), background `#FFFFFF`, cards `#F2F5F8`.

Every page has a title text box at the top left and a source line at the bottom (8 pt grey).

## Page 1. Overview

- Title: "HR Analytics: Attrition, Absence & Time to Hire"
- Subtitle: "Three separate datasets: IBM (fictional) attrition, UCI (real, other company) absence, SIMULATED recruiting"
- Row of 8 cards: Headcount, Attrition Rate, Avg Tenure (yrs), Absence Hours,
  Absence Hours per Employee per Year, Median Time to Hire (days, SIMULATED),
  Median Time to Fill (days, SIMULATED), Offer Acceptance Rate (SIMULATED). Cards for
  SIMULATED measures get an orange border and the label "SIMULATED".
- Below, three small visuals, one per area: Attrition Rate by dim_department[department_name]
  (bar), Absence Hours by dim_weekday[weekday_name] (column), Median Time to Hire by
  dim_date[year_month] via opened date (line, title "SIMULATED").
- Source line: "IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance (fictional dataset created
  by IBM data scientists); UCI Absenteeism at work (CC BY 4.0); recruiting data SIMULATED."

## Page 2. Attrition (IBM, fictional)

- Slicers (top): dim_department[department_name], dim_employee[gender], dim_employee[overtime]
- Cards: Headcount, Leavers, Attrition Rate, Avg Tenure Leavers (yrs)
- Clustered bar: Attrition Rate by dim_job_role[job_role], sorted descending, constant line =
  Company Attrition Rate (Analytics pane, or add the measure as a line)
- Bar: Attrition Rate by dim_employee[tenure_band]
- Matrix heatmap: rows dim_employee[overtime], columns dim_employee[income_band], value
  Attrition Rate, conditional formatting background colour scale white to `#E76F51`
- Bar: Attrition Rate by dim_employee[business_travel]
- Title of the page: "Attrition: overtime, low pay and the first two years"

## Page 3. Absence (UCI, real records, different company)

- Banner text: "Real absence records from a courier company in Brazil (Jul 2007 to Jul 2010).
  Not the IBM employees."
- Cards: Absence Hours, Absence Spells, Absence Hours per Employee per Year, Avg Hours per Spell
- Bar: Absence Hours by dim_absence_reason[reason_name], Top N 10 filter, legend
  dim_absence_reason[reason_group]
- Matrix heatmap: rows dim_absence_month[month_name], columns dim_weekday[weekday_name],
  value Absence Hours
- Table: dim_absence_employee[absence_employee_key], Absence Spells, Absence Days (8h),
  Bradford Factor (annual), sorted by Bradford descending, top 10
- Optional card "Absence Rate (estimated)" with the subtitle "assumes 8 h x 220 days"

## Page 4. Time to hire (SIMULATED)

- Banner text (orange): "SIMULATED data generated by src/simulate_hiring.py. Not real
  recruiting records."
- Slicers: dim_department[department_name], fact_candidate_SIMULATED[source]
- Cards: Requisitions (SIMULATED), Filled Requisitions (SIMULATED), Median Time to Hire
  (days, SIMULATED), Median Time to Fill (days, SIMULATED), Offer Acceptance Rate (SIMULATED)
- Funnel visual: Category fact_candidate_stage_SIMULATED[stage], value Candidates at Stage
  (SIMULATED)
- Line: Median Time to Hire and Median Time to Fill by dim_date[year] and [quarter_name]
  (opened date)
- Bar: Hires per 100 Applicants (SIMULATED) by fact_candidate_SIMULATED[source]
